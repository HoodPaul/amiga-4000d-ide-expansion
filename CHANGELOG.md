# Changelog

## Revision 0.1 - 26 October 2026

- Compact 58.42 × 55.88 mm four-layer PCB for the Amiga 4000 Desktop.
- Two 40-pin IDE outputs and a 34-pin floppy pass-through.
- Motherboard IDE/floppy sockets and two interrupt-isolation diodes underneath.
- Black-PCB appearance with white printing and underside UK Computing/JMC artwork.
- Top title: Amiga 4000D IDE Expansion. Port labels: IDE B / Secondary, IDE A / Primary and Floppy.
- Rear bottom-left revision label and connector references below the pin-1 labels.
- Printed labels at least 1 mm high, complete upper IDE B outline and pin-1 triangles concealed by the nominal connector bodies.
- Native KiCad check: zero DRC violations, zero unconnected pads and zero footprint errors.
- Copper, drills, outline and electrical mapping preserved during the final artwork changes.
- Paul reports the PCB package submitted for fabrication. Physical inspection, assembly, fit and functional testing remain pending.
- Initial GitHub project includes CAD, manufacturing files, BOM, previews, build documentation and AI handover instructions.
