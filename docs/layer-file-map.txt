Amiga 4000D IDE Expansion REV 0.1
Four copper layers, top to bottom:

L1  Amiga4000_IDE_Expansion-F_Cu.gtl     Top signals and pads
L2  Amiga4000_IDE_Expansion-In1_Cu.g2    Ground plane
L3  Amiga4000_IDE_Expansion-In2_Cu.g3    Ground plane
L4  Amiga4000_IDE_Expansion-B_Cu.gbl     Bottom signals and pads

F_Mask.gts         Top solder mask
B_Mask.gbs         Bottom solder mask
F_Silkscreen.gto   Top white printing
B_Silkscreen.gbo   Bottom white printing
Edge_Cuts.gm1      Board outline
PTH.drl           Plated drills
NPTH.drl          Non-plated drills (may contain no holes)

All names use the Amiga4000_IDE_Expansion- prefix in the manufacturing folder.
Nominal outline: 58.42 x 55.88 mm. Nominal thickness: 1.6 mm.
Select black solder mask and white silkscreen on the order form.
